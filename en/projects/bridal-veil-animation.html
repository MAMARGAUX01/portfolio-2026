The Bridal Veil, animating a legend

A waterfall that only appears at the end of the story: telling a legend from La Réunion through light.

Role
Art direction, 3D and animation
Tools
Blender, Procreate
Style reference
Michel Ocelot, Yann Nguema
01

Problem and context

The Bridal Veil (Voile de la Mariée) is a waterfall in Salazie, on La Réunion, and the starting point of a legend. This project animates it with a 3D set built in Blender and illustrated elements made in Procreate, referencing Michel Ocelot’s Princes and Princesses, but working with light on a dark background rather than shadows.

The same dark set serves the whole story, which takes place in the forest: places are suggested by sound (a church is bells) and by luminous silhouettes. The waterfall only appears at the very end, in full light.

Problem statement

How can a legend be told in animation with a single dark set, letting light suggest the places and the characters?

  • One set for the whole story: the forest.
  • Places are suggested by sound and by luminous silhouettes.
  • Every element lights the set, just as the waterfall does at the end.
02

Approach and analysis

The set

Cliff, waterfall and vegetation modelled in Blender, with a 120-frame sequence for the flow of the waterfall.

The light

A pink light for love, two fast, jerky halos for the chase, a glow that weakens and fades out at the fall.

The sound

Places are born from sound: pulsing bells for the church, before light draws the building.

How the scene unfolds

From left to right across the set: the lovers and the father’s prohibition, the wedding, the father with his sabre, the escape upwards, the fall from the top of the waterfall, then darkness, the glow of the father crying, and finally the waterfall.
